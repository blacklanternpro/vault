## v9.3: the revolver (p9g)
- **Asset:** Jake's GLB (`python-revolver-v2.glb`, one 40k-vert scanned mesh, 2048 PNG maps). `src/tools_prep_revolver.py` (run against the original) Taubin-smooths the relief of the frame mark and grip medallion, uses texture copies with every factory mark painted out (`dif_clean`/`nor_clean`, made earlier with per-region fills), stamps invented roll-marks into the diffuse by planar projection of barrel-side texels ("DROVER ARMS CO. / .357 MAGNUM CTG" right side, "DROVER MODEL 87 / BALLARAT VIC" left), and writes `revolver.glb` (1.86 MB, 1024 JPEG maps). Smoothing the barrel tore its UV islands, so the barrel is decal-only. The GLB is squashed in x: everything uses x × 1.2. Real scale ×0.126 (0.24 m). `build.py` embeds it like the hammer/saw (`GLB_ASSETS.revolver`, `url:` in the CDN build; `release.sh` copies the three GLBs to the vault root).
- **Parts at runtime (`gunSplitGLB`):** triangle centroids sort the mesh into cylinder (`hypot(x, y−0.306) < 0.162`, z −0.29..0.04), hammer, trigger and frame. `gunRig` hangs them on pivots in the GLB frame (`GP`: crane pivot (0.11, 0.165), hammer (0, 0.34, −0.43), trigger (0, 0.05, −0.24), muzzle (0, 0.41, 0.96)). Six instanced rims + noses show what's in each chamber. The sights are measured from the geometry; `gunAdsCalc` puts the notch and blade on the camera axis 0.40 m out. A RoomEnvironment PMREM goes on the gun's material (`envMapIntensity` follows the viewmodel light). The procedural stand-in (`gunProcGeo`) is used until or unless the GLB arrives (headless always).
- **The safe (`villaGun`, end of `buildVilla`):** BUNYIP 0.5 × 1.2 × 0.42 m against the WIR's north wall (`vFr(15.65,5.0,0,1)`), `bolted:true` (p2 `wakeEnt` now ignores bolted ents), `armor:true`. The kin door is hinged on the arch side. Locked → E says so. Three ways in: the key (cup hook above the robe shelving, south end, plan (17.6, 2.05, 7.37); `GUN.key`), the saw along the door (`door.sawTime=13`; p7's `onSawn` cut rate now honours `e.sawTime`), or the bar in the gap within 10 cm of either edge (`door.onLever`; p7 `crowLever` gives ents with `onLever` a free fulcrum and hands them the work; 13 work with a slip-and-reseat every 4; the door bulges as it goes). The ammo flap (top-hinged, swings up 166°) has its own lock: the same key, a ≥2.5 power hit, a 4 s cut or 2.5 lever work. Inside are KESTREL .357 × 50, the revolver on foam on the 0.6 m shelf, and Gary's note. The earmuffs hang on the T-handle (the door's `use(h)` checks `h.object`). Two new Gary notices go in `VNOTES`.
- **Handling (`gunTool` from `updateTools` tool 5):** six-chamber state `GUN.ch` ('', 'L', 'S'), `GUN.idx` = the chamber under the hammer. LMB = DA: 0.26 s pull, the cylinder indexes at 75% (`pullC`), fires at 100%, and letting go early does nothing. Q (keydown via `inKey`, so the phone stick's sprint `keys.KeyQ` can't cock it) = cock (index + hammer back) / ease down. A cocked LMB breaks at once. R tap = open / load one into the next empty from idx+1 / close when full or the pocket's empty. Hold R ≥ 0.45 s = eject: empties become `.357 case (fired)` props, live rounds go back to `GUN.ammo`. LMB while open = close. RMB = sights (`GUN.adsK` → camera FOV 78 → 58 via `gunFov()` in p8a, look speed −35%, walk bob and mouse sway mostly off, crosshair hidden). Sprint is off with the gun out (p6). Six slots: `toolCount()`; `setTool` wraps by it; HUD slot 6 shows once taken; `#gunhud` reads the chambers in firing order + pocket + DA/COCKED + MUFFS.
- **Firing (`gunFire`):** spread σ = 0.0011 + hip 0.010 + DA jerk 0.0035 + moving 0.006 (crouch × 0.75), plus a breathing sway that also moves the viewmodel. Recoil: the view climbs 2.9–4° for good (you bring it down) and the viewmodel flips ~24 rad/s. The flash is a viewmodel card + `VL.flare` (26 for 50 ms, same guards as SPK) + viewmodel light spike + smoke + 4 sparks. **`onSpark` at the muzzle** (a gassy room goes up). `gunBang`: crack + body + boom; inside, five slapback taps; outside, three rolling echoes.
- **Hearing:** `gunEars` inserts a low-pass + gain between `S.comp` and the speakers and adds a 6.9/7.35 kHz ringing pair that bypasses them. Unprotected shot: `deaf` +0.8, `ring` +0.85; cutoff 20 k → ~280 Hz at deaf 1; recovery over 5 s + 0.45 s per recent shot; ring fades over 9 s. With muffs (`M`): everything ≤ 1.3 kHz and ×0.6, shots ×0.3 and no ring.
- **Ballistics (`gunTrace`):** a forward walk of `castRay(…,{ray, near:0.0004})` (castRay grew `near`) spends an energy budget of 1.0 as it goes (`gunMatOf`: plasterboard 0.05 per sheet, which is about 0.1 per stud wall; stud 0.2; brick 1.5; slab, safe and screws 9; props by material and mass). A plasterboard hit wakes its dormant wall first so the studs are there. Hard surfaces at grazing incidence (cos < 0.25) ricochet (E × 0.35, a whine, up to 3 bounces). Then a backward walk over the same stretch finds the far sides (exit blowouts). Both run up to 48 steps. Off the hit tree, `gunEnv` uses cannon's `raycastAll` against `NS.body` (houses = brick, fences = pass through) and the ground plane outside the house (road or dirt, ricochets). Effects (`gunEnter`/`gunExit`): sheet holes + blowouts (and a 2 px blot in the canvas; enough holes in one sheet punch it out), splinters, a brick strike chips the face and a second knocks the brick out (`chipBrick`), pipes leak (gas too), cables cut, glass/screens/cans via `damageProp(2.6)`, the gas bottle gets `holed`, steel sparks. Bond: $40 per wall per shot (`GUN.shotWalls`).
- **Holes (`GDEC`):** one 600-instance decal mesh (tinted per material) for the house; things that move get child cards (`gdecProp`, ≤10 each, unbatched first). `gdecSweep` hides house decals whose owner died, moved or fell off. Decals and child cards don't raycast.
- **Neighbours:** a shot outside (or the 2nd inside): two barks, then `NS.dogQuiet` = 600 s. Outside or the 3rd inside: the porch light across the road comes on after 4 s and stays on 3 min (`GUN.wokeT`; p9n's off-switch waits for it).
- **Phone:** ACT = FIRE, ALT = SIGHTS, `mR` (CYL/LOAD) and `mQ` (HAMMER) show with the gun out; the radial gets REVOLVER once taken.

## v9.1: darts (p9d)
- **Board:** regulation (bull 6.35 mm, outer bull 15.9, treble 99–107, double 162–170, edge 225.5 mm radius; `DR`, `DORDER` from the 20 clockwise), a 1024² canvas face (`dartTex`), centre at 1.73 m on the garage's east wall (`vFr(3.2,7.6,-1,0)`), in a timber cabinet whose two open doors are chalkboards (`DART.doors[i].drawable`, texta works on them). `DART.B` = {c centre on the face, n out, r right, u up}. `dartScore(p)` → {seg, mult, pts, label, wire (distance to the nearest wire), r}.
- **Oche:** tape 2.37 m out (`DART.oche`); `SEATS.oche` (E on board/cabinet/chalkboards → `sitDown('oche')`): look ±25°/±17° (`SEAT.ly/lp`), view sways with breathing × `dartDrunkK()`, fov 64, the crosshair stays on (p7 `updateReticle` exception). The hand dart is a child of the camera.
- **Throw (`dartThrowOche`):** draw time td from LMB down to up. Sweet spot 0.4–0.7 s (narrows/shifts later with drink); early = weak (falls), late = a little strong + extra scatter; <0.12 s = a lob. `dartAimVel(p0,A,pf,σ)` solves the launch so a clean throw lands 0.11·(D/2.4) m under the aim point (darts drop: aim high), then scales by pf and scatters by σ (0.0034 rad sober). Measured headless: 24 clean throws aimed 11 cm over the bull land with mean (1,−10) mm, spread ~13–16 mm.
- **Flight (`dartFly`):** 3 substeps a frame, each a `castRay(len,{ray:{o,d}})` (castRay grew a `ray` option) from the last position. `dartHit`: the board → stick (1 mm wire = 35% bounce-out) + score; brick/slab/tile/metal/glass/ceramic/plastic/screens → bounce, becomes a loose physics dart (`dartLoose`, max 10, `isDart`); everything else (plaster, timber, chipboard, fabric, foam) → stick; plaster gets a hole blot + $15 bond. Stuck darts ride their ent (`d.lm` local matrix) and fall out as loose darts if it breaks/detaches/moves.
- **Rendering:** all darts are two `InstancedMesh`es (body with vertex colours, flights with instance colour: you red, Dazza blue, free yellow), `DART.cap` 48, oldest stray off the board goes first.
- **Games (`DART.game`):** practice / 501 double out (bust on <0, 1, or 0 without a double; `checkout(rem)` finds the 1–3 dart out) / Around the Clock (1–20 then bull, timer) / Cricket v Dazza (15–20 + bull, 3 marks to close, extra marks score while the other hasn't closed). A turn ends after 3 scored darts (`G.tn`) or a bust → state `pull` → LMB pulls them. Dazza (`dazzaTurn/dazzaPick/dazzaThrow`): picks a target (scores where he's closed and you haven't when he's behind, else closes the highest), aims the treble centre with σ 17 mm and throws a real dart from 0.4 m left of the oche (exact solve, no drop); chirps via HUD lines "[DAZZA, THROUGH THE ROLLER DOOR] …". Bests and the Dazza tally save in localStorage (`bedsit.darts`). Use `after()` (game clock), never `setTimeout`, for turn timing (headless runs don't advance real time).
- **SWILL:** hands + a can in hand + LMB = `swillSip` (p7); 3 sips = an empty can, `DART.drunk` +1 (decays 1 per 4 min). The darts case on the bench hands you a loose dart; RMB with it = `dartThrowHeld` (a real ray-traced throw at whatever you're looking at).
- **Also:** the boxing bag moved to plan (1.6, 6.05), out of the line of flight. Phone: at the oche ACT = hold to draw/throw (PULL DARTS when due), USE = next game, STAND.
- **VCULL fix (p9z):** `vcRoomsOf` cached a room from a never-rendered mesh's identity matrix (anything built after the first render, or positioned after mkEnt, could be hidden forever). It now updates the matrix before the first lookup.

### Key EXECUTIONER facts (v2 shipped in v5.3; see p8f below)
- **Cell:**
  - Cell spans x −3..3, z −2.5..4.5, ceiling 3.0. The player camera sits at (0, 1.62, 3.3), facing −z.
  - The window wall is at z −2.5. The window opening is x ±2.2, y 1.05–2.45, with glass at z −2.68. The piers are 0.8 m wide at x ±2.6.
  - The prisoner kneels at (0, 0, 0.25).
- **Observation room:**
  - It spans z −2.85..−6.2. The console sits at z −3.08, 0.9 m high, with three monitors.
  - The locked door is in the back wall at x 2.25.
  - Scientists stand at z about −3.5 (Voss −1.35, Hale 0.05, Ames 1.4). Their panic targets are hide (−0.92, −3.42), door (1.92, −5.72) and hands-up (0.7, −5.85).
- **Ragdolls:** humans become ragdolls in `EX.world`, a separate cannon world. Static env boxes go in via `exStaticBox`. Ragdoll parts are collision group 2, mask 1, so parts don't collide with each other.
- **Raycasts:** call `EX.scene.updateMatrixWorld()` before raycasting. `exTrace` does this; there was a bug before it did.
- **cannon friction scales with the contact count, and per step:** each contact gets a friction impulse cap of μ·g·m *per step* (not per second), so a bed on four legs over two carpet strips (~50 contacts) would not slide at μ 0.45 or even 0.008. Things meant to be shoved get `matShove` (μ 0.0001 against the default material; p9r).
- **Wall frames:** before v6.9 `vFr` measured from the wall's plan line, which is one face of the stud wall (walls sit on the +x / +z side of their line, 0.11 thick; exterior walls' inner face is the line). Anything on the far side sat 11 cm inside the wall and jammed the moment it woke. `vFaceOff` fixes it for every `vFr` call.
- **A Drawable's `blot(u,v,r,rgb,a)`** takes 0–1 UVs; for the floor use `floorDrawableAt(x,z)` + `floorUV(x,z,d)`.
- **Speech:** the PA uses `speechSynthesis` only when seated or within 3 m.

## Mobile (v5.5, p9m)
- **Detection:** `TOUCH` in p2 (`(pointer:coarse)`, or touch events plus `maxTouchPoints`). `const TOUCH_ON=true` is the kill switch: set it `false` and the game behaves exactly like desktop.
- **Phone tier:** `QTIER='phone'` on touch. Pixel ratio is capped at 1.5, the shadow map is 1024 with one shadow light, minor lamps are off, `mkDrawable` canvases are capped at about 1 Mpx and the TV ad channel renders at 640×360. The panel's QUALITY button cycles phone → full → perf (`qualityCycle`).
- **Input:** everything goes through the p7 entry points `inKey/inDown/inUp/inLook/inPull/inWheel`, so touch never re-implements game logic.
  - Left half of the screen is a floating stick that drives `keys.W/A/S/D` (pushed to the rim it sprints).
  - Right half is look-drag (`inLook`).
  - Holding ACT or ALT turns a right-half drag into the pull for prying and ripping (`inPull`; look freezes while held).
  - Mouse events are ignored on touch unless the pointer is locked, because phones fire synthetic mouse events after touches.
- **Buttons** (`#mob`, contextual via `mobileRelabel` every frame):
  - Walking: ACT (LMB), ALT (RMB), USE (E), KICK, JUMP, CROUCH, TOOL (tap cycles, hold opens the radial picker), ≡ panel.
  - Couch: CH ▲ / SQUINT / STAND / CH ▼ / PWR / VOL.
  - Desk: FIRE / SIGHTS / RELOAD / USE (F, shown when the prompt is up) / STAND / ≡ (Tab menu; then SELECT and the stick move it).
- **Entering:** the overlay tap calls `mobileEnter` (Android fullscreen + landscape lock; iOS ignores both, so a rotate card shows in portrait).
- **Untested on a real iPhone/Android.** `pw_mob.py` and `pw_mob3.py` run Chromium phone emulation (844×390, touch, CDP touch events) only.

## The villa (v6, p9v)
- **Flag:** `VILLA` (p2) is true for `?villa` or `window.__VILLA`. p7 `buildWorld` calls `buildVilla()` instead of the bedsit; the loop calls `updateVilla(dt)`; the fridge loop is skipped. p3 empties `WALLS` after `mkCeiling` in villa mode.
- **Plan:** offset ox −8.8, oz −4.9 (plan metres → world). Rooms in p9v `VP.rooms`; the built layout differs from the doc's first drawing (entry corridor 1.2 m wide, office at the front, bath lobby with the WC and basin). Internal walls are trimmed 0.11 at T-junctions.
- **Dormant walls (the big perf idea):** studs, plates, noggins, batts and screws exist as data only, with no body, mesh or screw instance (`mkEnt` honours `dormant`). The first time a wall is opened up (`detachChunk`, `shardChunk`, `liftChunk`, a saw kerf) `villaWake(w)` registers its bodies, builds a per-wall StaticBatch (`batchRef`) and instances its screws. One wake ≈ +23 bodies, +172 screws.
- **Lazy screws:** `addScrew` stores screws with `id:-1` on dormant walls; `screwInstance` gives them an InstancedMesh slot later. Raycasts map instance ids through `SCREW_IDX`. `SCREW_MAX` is 16000 in villa mode.
- **Lights:** a HemisphereLight (ground colour = fake bounce light; without it every ceiling and door head far from a lamp goes black), ambient, a dim moon, and a pool of four PointLights that hop to the fixtures nearest you every 0.15 s (`pool[0]` always sits in your room and is the only shadow caster).
- **Load numbers (empty house):** 317 bodies (bedsit 775), 46 walls, 73 faces, 2,627 sheet chunks, 788 dormant timber pieces, 160 batts, 7,743 screws (0 instanced), 10.2 Mpx of canvas, 60–248 draw calls depending on the view, 104k–192k triangles, headless boot about 3–4 s (bedsit about 11 s). 0 chunks fall at load.

## Gotchas learned the hard way
- **The sofa's backrest was on the TV side until v5.1** (frame back part at local z +0.325 while the cushions sat as if facing west). v5.1 moved it to −0.325 and resized the cushions so nothing overlaps; a wake no longer kicks them off.
- **Shared module scope:**
  - Duplicate top-level names are a SyntaxError (`_m4`, `_vx` already clashed; p8b uses `_hvx`/`_hm4`).
  - Duplicate `function` declarations also break; replace a function, never redeclare it.
  - After every build, run `node --check all.mjs`.
- **Sleeping cannon bodies** report garbage constraint multipliers. Skip force checks on sleeping bodies.
- **Thin rods** need fattened colliders and mass.
- **Headless aiming:** after setting yaw/pitch, wait frames before raycasting. Camera shake moves the aim between hits.
- **Screen glass:** glass with `screenHit` routes through `crackScreen` before the generic glass branch, in `damageProp` and the kick.
- **node-canvas** has no `Path2D` and may lack `roundRect`. Use the `rrect` helper and plain path functions.
- **Don't `pkill -f <script name>`:** it can kill your own shell. Kill by PID. Same trap with `until ! pgrep -f "x.py"` wait loops: the loop's own command line matches, so it waits forever. Match `"python3 x.py"` or wait on a log line instead.
- **Swiftshader + 2 cores:** a full `pw_perf8.py` sweep takes ~20 min; run it in the background and do other work. `runall.sh` (26 harnesses) takes ~10 min.
- **Speech:** `speechSynthesis` isn't routable through Web Audio, so it is not positional. Keep it to seated or near-device use.
